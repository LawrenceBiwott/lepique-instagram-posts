Lepique Auto-Poster

Lepique Auto-Poster is a content publishing tool for fashion retailers. It helps authorized users connect their TikTok accounts, upload approved product videos, and publish marketing content through TikTok’s official API.

The app is designed for fashion businesses that manage their own approved product videos and want a simple publishing workflow for TikTok content.

How It Works

Authorized users can use Lepique Auto-Poster to prepare fashion marketing videos, connect an approved TikTok account, and publish selected content using TikTok’s official developer tools.
