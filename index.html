Lepique Studio is a web-based content publishing tool used by Lepique Fashions, a ladies' jeans and everyday-fashion retailer based in Nairobi, Kenya, to review and publish approved product videos to its connected social media accounts, including TikTok.

What Lepique Studio Does

Lepique Studio connects an authorized TikTok creator account and allows Lepique Fashions to review a queue of approved product photos and videos before they are published. Once a piece of content is approved, Lepique Studio publishes it to the connected TikTok account on the retailer's behalf.

How It Uses TikTok's APIs

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic scope) authenticates the authorized TikTok account and reads basic public profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) so Lepique Studio knows which account it is publishing to.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload and video.publish scopes) uploads approved product videos and publishes them directly to the authorized account's TikTok profile.
