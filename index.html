Our Story

Where It's All About Quality

Lepique Fashions is Nairobi's premier destination for ladies' denim. Located at Platinum Plaza on Tom Mboya Street, we've built a reputation for stocking the latest, most flattering jeans styles at prices every woman can afford.

From wide-leg to high waist, from light wash to deep indigo — our collection is refreshed weekly so there's always something new to discover. We believe every woman deserves jeans that fit perfectly and feel amazing.

How to Order

Getting your favourite jeans delivered to your door is quick and easy.

1

Browse Styles

Check our full catalogue above or follow us on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok for new arrivals.

2

Choose & Message

Pick your style, colour and size then send us a WhatsApp message at 0721 672 900.

3

Confirm & Pay

We'll confirm availability, share your total, and you pay via M-Pesa. Fast & secure.

4

We Deliver!

Your order is packaged and dispatched. Nairobi same-day or countrywide via courier.
