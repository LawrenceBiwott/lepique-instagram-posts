This Privacy Policy explains how Lepique Studio, operated by Lepique Fashions ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when an authorized user connects a TikTok account to the Service.

2. How We Use This Information

We use this information solely to operate Lepique Studio: to confirm which TikTok account is authorized, and to publish approved product content to that account. We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to make authorized API calls to TikTok. We retain the minimum information necessary to operate the Service and remove it if authorization is revoked or no longer needed.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share the information described above with third parties, except as required to operate the Service through TikTok's own APIs, or where required by law.

5. Your Controls

  • You may revoke Lepique Studio's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's account settings.
  • You may request that we delete any information we hold about your authorized connection by contacting us below.

6. Children's Privacy

Lepique Studio is intended for use by authorized Lepique Fashions staff and is not directed at children.
