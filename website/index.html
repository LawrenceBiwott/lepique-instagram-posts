Policy

Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to love your purchase. Here's everything you need to know.

✅ Eligible for Exchange

  • Exchanged within 72 hours of purchase
  • Item is unworn and unwashed
  • Item is clean and undamaged
  • Item is in its original condition

❌ Not Eligible for Exchange

  • Sale or discounted items
  • Worn, washed or altered items
  • Damaged or stained items
  • Requests made after 72 hours
Please note: We offer exchanges only — no cash refunds. Size exchanges depend on stock availability. Delivery fees are non-refundable, and customers are responsible for delivery costs during exchanges.

To request an exchange, WhatsApp us on 0721 672 900 or visit us at Platinum Plaza, Shop G16, Tom Mboya Street, Nairobi CBD.
