Lepique Auto-Poster Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

Lepique Auto-Poster is a content publishing tool operated by Lepique Fashions. The app helps authorized fashion retailers connect their TikTok accounts, upload approved product videos, and publish marketing content through TikTok’s official API.

Information We Collect

Lepique Auto-Poster may access basic TikTok account information required for authentication, such as account identification details provided through TikTok Login. The app may also access video upload and publishing permissions when authorized by the user.

How We Use Information

We use information only to authenticate authorized users, connect TikTok accounts, upload approved videos, and publish selected marketing content through TikTok’s official API.

Content Data

Videos uploaded through Lepique Auto-Poster are product or marketing videos provided, owned, or approved by the authorized user or fashion retailer using the app.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. We only share information where necessary to operate the app, comply with legal obligations, or use TikTok’s official API services.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect app-related information. Access tokens and credentials are used only for authorized app functions and should be kept secure.

User Control

Users may disconnect or revoke TikTok access through their TikTok account settings or by contacting Lepique Fashions.
